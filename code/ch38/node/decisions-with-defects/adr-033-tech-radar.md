# ADR-033: Morada's tech radar, reviewed every quarter

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-20
- Decided by: the architecture forum
- Supersedes: ADR-030

## Context

Each team picked libraries and databases on its own, and Morada ended up with three
ways of accessing queues.

## Decision

A tech radar with four rings, reviewed every quarter by the architecture forum.

## Alternatives considered

- A list of approved technologies: it becomes a gate and ages quickly.

## Consequences

The teams know what is recommended and what to avoid in new systems.

## When to revisit

If the radar stops being consulted in change proposals.
