Quiet hours are from 10 p.m. to 8 a.m., every day, and during that time no noise, loud music, or construction work is allowed.

The party room must be reserved at least 15 days in advance, through the app. The party room reservation fee is $180.00, billed on the next month's invoice.

Cleaning the party room after use is the responsibility of the resident who made the reservation, by 10 a.m. the next day.

Small pets are allowed, as long as they are carried or kept on a leash in the common areas.

Move-ins and move-outs are permitted only on weekdays, from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m., with 48-hour notice to the office.

The pool is open from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. Each unit may bring up to four guests to the pool, accompanied by a resident.
